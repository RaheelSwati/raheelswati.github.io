Dragon Robot Pro: Rift Trials
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy applies specifically to Dragon Robot Pro: Rift Trials, the paid, ad-free Pro game published by Race Storm Studio on Google Play and Amazon Appstore. It does not describe the advertising-supported Free edition or other studio apps.

Data handled by the game

The Pro game does not require an account or login. Race Storm Studio does not collect or transmit personal data from this version of the game. It does not contain active advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics tracking, chat, social networking, or in-app purchases.

Game progress, earned coins, upgrades, settings, medals, and trial records are stored locally on your device. The game does not provide a studio-operated cloud-save service. Device or store backup behavior is controlled by your platform and its settings.

Permissions and offline play

Gameplay works offline after installation. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or media library. Internet access is available for optional external links; no account or personal information is required to play.

Optional external links and store purchases

If you choose More Games, this Privacy Policy link, or a store link, your browser or store app opens an external service. That service may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information under its own privacy policy. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not use our own cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels.

The platform where you purchase or download the game handles payment, licensing, delivery, and any platform account information. Race Storm Studio does not receive your payment-card details through the game. Please review Google's Privacy Policy, Amazon's Privacy Notice, and GitHub's Privacy Statement for their services.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal data collected by this Pro game because it does not collect such data. Local progress remains until the app's stored data is cleared or the app is removed, subject to platform backup settings. Clearing stored data resets progress and cannot be undone by us.

Teen audience

This game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

Contact and policy changes

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. Do not include passwords or payment-card details. Contact Race Storm Studio at raheelswati1@gmail.com.

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated with a revised effective date and the relevant store disclosures will be updated.
