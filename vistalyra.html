Which video files can I use?

Common containers include MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM and MPEG transport streams. Compatibility depends on the video/audio codec, resolution, bitrate and headset. Ask us about your file if you need help choosing the right setup.

Which advanced formats are available today?

Version 1.0.1 adds decoder paths for compatible MVC, MV-HEVC and AV1 files, plus experimental unencrypted local single-clip UDF ISO playback. Protected discs, disc menus and multi-clip playlists are not supported. Selected samples were checked on Quest 2; broad codec, sustained high-resolution and real NAS/USB compatibility still need further testing. WebDAV and independent width/height correction are planned for the next update, subject to compatibility testing. They are not included in this release; no release date is confirmed.

Meta Quest: getting started

  1. Keep the headset on while opening the app. Allow video access to browse local media, or select a file with Open file.
  2. Use the viewing-mode button to choose projection and stereo separately. Automatic mode reads supported filename tags and stereo metadata.
  3. A mono video remains mono in a sphere. For stereo depth, use an actual side-by-side or over-under stereo source and its matching mode.

Comfortable controls

  • Hold grip away from the interface and move the controller to orient the picture. Twist your wrist to correct tilt.
  • Use the thumbstick to seek and zoom. Hold grip with the stick to change flat-screen distance.
  • Point at a panel and hold grip to reposition that panel. Press X to recenter.
  • Press the index trigger away from buttons to show or hide controls; point at a button and press the trigger to select it. In rooms, B/Y selects a pointed seat. Controls fade during playback when idle; interacting keeps them visible.

Background removal

Compatible tagged SBS fisheye-alpha files use their embedded matte automatically. Green and blue detection is conservative and runs locally. For other compatible backgrounds, choose the green, blue, black or gray key in passthrough settings and adjust its threshold. Ordinary footage without an embedded matte or suitable background cannot reliably become a cutout.

Troubleshooting

If startup remains on the system loading dots, close and reopen Vistalyra. If the headset or other apps are also slow, restart the headset. For incorrect depth, check projection, stereo packing and eye order. Codec support depends on the headset and file.

This release does not support DRM services, protected discs, disc menus, multi-clip ISO playlists or proprietary lens calibration. NAS/USB compatibility and sustained high-resolution playback still need broader checks. Videos are not bundled. Use Quit in Library or Settings to close the app.
