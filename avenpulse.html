Archived developer journal · 12 October 2026

This journal and the preview above document earlier development, not the current release status. Avenpulse released on Meta Quest on October 17, 2026. Open the current store listing.

Making the first few sessions easier to start.

The app began with short hand-led rhythm sessions. The current source now also includes guided seated and standing classes, and we’ve been working on how a beginner finds their first session and understands each demonstration.

A path with a clear next visit

The four-week beginner programme contains twelve visits. It mixes introductory movement, mobility and rhythm sessions, with seated alternatives where available. Progress is saved locally, so someone can return to an earlier week without erasing completed visits.

Before moving, the app presents real-space checks. A virtual chair or floor marker cannot confirm that someone’s real room is clear. Watch mode lets people see a demonstration without adding participation time, and class timing stops during pauses, focus loss and required tracking loss.

Front and side views, matching words

The new preview shows the coach’s movement library from two angles. We’ve refined seated support, leg extensions, hand placement and transitions between movement and rest. Both coach appearances have matching local narration; the video presents one of those voice packs.

The source offers slower pacing, smaller movement range, repeat instruction, warm-up, recovery and cooldown phases. Our next reviews need to establish how these feel to beginners wearing a headset.

What this update can show

The footage shows the implemented development source. Desktop compilation and programme checks have passed, but they do not establish sustained headset frame rate, stereo clarity, comfort or exercise suitability.

Exercise-content review, beginner usability testing and headset performance work remain open. This app does not assess lower-body form or provide validated calorie estimates. No clinical, weight-loss or spot-fat-loss result is being claimed.

The feedback we’d value

What would help you follow a short seated or standing session? A slower demonstration, another camera angle, clearer wording, or a different way to see your next visit? We’re interested in those practical details as the app develops.
