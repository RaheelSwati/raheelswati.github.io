Game and support

Nut Sort VR: Bolt's Workshop

Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026.

Extrude Studio, publishing as Extrude Gaming, is responsible for this single-player VR puzzle game and this privacy policy. Contact: raheelswati1@gmail.com.

Headset and controller input

The game processes headset and controller positions, rotations, button presses and interaction inputs during play to display the VR view, move virtual hands and interact with objects. These inputs are used in memory for gameplay; the game does not upload them to a developer server or use them for advertising or profiling.

Saved progress

The game stores puzzle progress, earned coins, unlocks and preferences locally on your headset so you can resume your progress and use your chosen settings. It has no developer-operated account service or cloud leaderboard. Platform backup features may handle app data according to your Meta settings.

Optional microphone

Voice commands are optional. The game requests microphone permission when you use the voice control. Recognition runs locally on the headset. The game does not upload microphone recordings to a speech service or save them as audio files. Short audio buffers are processed during the listening session. You can use the buttons instead and revoke microphone permission in headset settings.

Platform services

The paid Meta build uses Meta's Platform SDK to verify ownership. Meta processes purchases, platform accounts and platform diagnostics under its own privacy policy. The game does not receive your payment-card details. Meta privacy policy.

Mixed-age players

The game is intended for mixed ages, including Meta-supported child accounts. The Meta release requests the current account's age category once per session as required by Meta. The game does not store this category or include it in technical logs. All categories receive the same local gameplay, without social networking, advertising or in-app checkout. An unavailable age-category response does not block gameplay.

Advertising and purchases

This release contains no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Coins and building parts are earned by playing and cannot be exchanged for money.

Diagnostics and contact

Local technical logs may contain app errors, microphone availability and accepted command identifiers. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending private recordings or unrelated personal information.

Deleting data

Use the headset's application-data controls or uninstall the game to remove local data. Platform backups and purchase records are controlled separately by Meta. All users in every region, including a parent or guardian acting for a child, can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing raheelswati1@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion. We do not charge a fee. Include only the details needed to identify the correspondence; do not send passwords or identity documents. If we must retain limited information to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a legal dispute, we will explain the reason and retain only what is necessary. We cannot delete records held independently by Meta; please use Meta account and privacy controls for those records.
