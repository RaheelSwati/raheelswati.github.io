Explore, craft and build

Edge of Survival: Sandbox is an open-world island survival game. Gather resources, craft equipment and build a base as you explore. The iPhone and iPad listing uses the title Untamed Survival Land.

Android: Edge of Survival: Sandbox

  • Explore an open island with the updated world map.
  • Use the grappling hook, hoverboards and vehicles to move around.
  • Gather materials, craft tools and weapons, and build a base.

View Edge of Survival: Sandbox on Google Play

iPhone and iPad: Untamed Survival Land

  • Explore five distinct islands, each with different resources and challenges.
  • Craft tools and weapons, build a base, and take on missions.
  • Play offline, as described on the App Store listing.
