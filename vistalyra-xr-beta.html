Help make movie controls comfortable on Galaxy XR

Vistalyra's Android XR beta plays your own 2D, side-by-side or over-under 3D, VR180 and 360 videos. Galaxy XR testers found that the blue panel still covered the movie after minimising. Build 5 uses a separate compact playback bar below the centered video, with Play/Pause, Show controls and Hide controls. Hide removes the whole panel; tap the video to bring controls back. These changes passed emulator checks and now need Galaxy XR retesting.

A short test is enough

  1. Open one of your own local videos and watch for a few minutes. Can you see the movie clearly with the compact controls, then use Hide controls and tap the video to show them again?
  2. Try moving the control panel by dragging its window border, seeking, and opening Projection, Stereo and Settings. Do selections work comfortably?
  3. Switch to another app and return. Does Vistalyra stay open with your video selected? Email a few lines about what worked or failed, with headset software version and steps to reproduce any problem.
