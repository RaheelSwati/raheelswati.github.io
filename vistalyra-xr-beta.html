Help make movie controls comfortable on Galaxy XR

Vistalyra's Android XR beta plays your own 2D, side-by-side or over-under 3D, VR180 and 360 videos. Galaxy XR testers found that the blue panel still covered the movie after minimising. The beta retains a separate compact playback bar below the centered video, with Play/Pause, Show controls and Hide controls. Hide removes the whole panel; tap the video to bring controls back. The beta also includes separate width/height correction for flat videos, saved per movie, and a read-only WebDAV media library. Controls, app-switch recovery and authenticated playback/seeking passed emulator checks; Galaxy XR and real NAS retesting remain necessary.

A short test is enough

  1. Open one of your own local videos and watch for a few minutes. Can you see the movie clearly with the compact controls, then use Hide controls and tap the video to show them again?
  2. Try moving the control panel by dragging its window border, seeking, and opening Projection, Stereo and Settings. Do selections work comfortably?
  3. Switch to another app and return. Does Vistalyra stay open with your video selected? Email a few lines about what worked or failed, with headset software version and steps to reproduce any problem.

In the latest beta, Settings has a saved Menu size adjustment. Try a comfortable size, reopen the app and check that it stays saved. The compact playback bar stays small.
Optional: in Settings, adjust Picture width/height for a stretched flat video, then reopen it to check the saved proportions. If you already use WebDAV, try its Library button; use HTTPS for passwords. No server setup is required for the basic controls test. If a WebDAV folder is still waiting, use Cancel loading; you can then retry without restarting the app.
