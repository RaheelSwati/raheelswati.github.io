Help make movie controls comfortable on Galaxy XR

Vistalyra's Android XR beta plays your own 2D, side-by-side or over-under 3D, VR180 and 360 videos. One tester found the controls covered the movie. Build 4 makes them smaller, starts them lower and minimises them when a movie opens. We need Galaxy XR owners to check this fix.

A short test is enough

  1. Open one of your own local videos and watch for a few minutes. Can you see the movie clearly with the controls open and minimised?
  2. Try moving the control panel with its handle, seeking, and opening Projection, Stereo and Settings. Do selections work comfortably?
  3. Email a few lines about what worked or failed, with headset software version and steps to reproduce any problem.

Honest feedback, including bugs, is helpful. No purchase, rating or public review is required. Recordings and diagnostics are optional; do not send private films or surroundings.
